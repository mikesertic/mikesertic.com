Newsletter

A short letter on self-government in practice.

Once a month I write about what it actually takes for people and organizations to govern themselves: what I am learning at the Advocates, what the Quiz and Political DNA data show, and what companies that run without managers can teach anyone who leads a team.

It is written for people in the liberty movement, for leaders curious about self-management, and for anyone who wants to think more clearly about their own politics. No fundraising asks. No forwarding of press releases.

  • One idea, worked throughA single question about self-government, answered with examples instead of slogans.
  • Field notes from the AdvocatesWhat is working in liberty education, from classrooms to street outreach.
  • Self-management in practiceLessons from Morning Star and other organizations without a chain of command.
  • What the data saysPatterns from the World's Smallest Political Quiz and Political DNA.
